India

About

India, officially known as the Republic of India, is a country located in South Asia. It ranks as the seventh-largest nation in the world by area and, since 2023, has become the most populous country, as well as the largest democracy since gaining independence in 1947. India is surrounded by the Indian Ocean to the south, the Arabian Sea to the southwest, and the Bay of Bengal to the southeast. It shares its land borders with Pakistan in the west, China, Nepal, and Bhutan in the north, and Bangladesh and Myanmar in the east. Across the Indian Ocean, India lies close to Sri Lanka and the Maldives, while the Andaman and Nicobar Islands extend its maritime boundaries toward Myanmar, Thailand, and Indonesia.

History

Around 55,000 years ago, the first modern humans (Homo sapiens) made their way from Africa to the Indian subcontinent. The earliest remains of these modern humans found in South Asia date back to roughly 30,000 years ago. By around 6500 BCE, early communities in places such as Mehrgarh (in present-day Balochistan, Pakistan) began showing signs of a settled lifestyle — cultivating crops, domesticating animals, building permanent homes, and storing surplus food. Over time, these developments laid the foundation for the rise of the Indus Valley Civilization, one of the world's earliest urban cultures. Flourishing between 2500 and 1900 BCE, this civilization spanned regions of modern-day Pakistan and western India. Its major cities — including Mohenjo-daro, Harappa, Dholavira, and Kalibangan — were remarkable for their urban planning, craftsmanship, and active trade networks that reached far beyond South Asia.
